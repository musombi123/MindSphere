## Important limitations

The uploaded video contains ordinary handheld camera frames, not a 360-degree panorama. The sky module uses curved, overlapping photo panels with feathered borders instead of pretending a standard frame is a true panorama or HDRI. Normal and roughness maps are derived from the luminance/detail of the same photos; they are not separate photographs.

The video frame stills are compressed for offline delivery and distant views. They are good source references for the look, but larger source frames or isolated tree/house/fence images would improve close-up texture detail.
